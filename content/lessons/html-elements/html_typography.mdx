<Step submit="Continue">

Typography is how text looks and feels on a page. Headings, paragraphs, quotes,
and inline marks all come from a small family of HTML tags. Let's meet them one
by one.

<CodeBlock code={"<h1>Main Title</h1>\n<h2>Subheading</h2>\n<h3>Section Title</h3>"} language="HTML" />

</Step>

<Step submit="Continue">

<CodeHighlight>{"<h6>"}</CodeHighlight> rank your sections. Arrange them in order.

<Step submit="Continue">

Paragraphs carry your body text. The <CodeHighlight>{"<p>"}</CodeHighlight> tag wraps
a block of sentences into one neat paragraph.

<Browser content="<p>This is a sample paragraph of text.</p>" title="Paragraph" />

<PracticeQuiz
  answer="<p>This is a sample paragraph of text.</p>"
  options={[
    { label: "<p>", priority: 0 },
    { label: "This is a sample paragraph of text.", priority: 1 },
    { label: "</p>", priority: 2 },
  ]}
  response={{
    positive: "Well done!",
    negative: "That's not correct, try again!",
  }}
  title={["Arrange the paragraph element in the correct order"]}
/>

</Step>

<Step submit="Continue">

For quotes or excerpts, use the <CodeHighlight>{"<blockquote>"}</CodeHighlight> tag
to set the text apart from the rest.

<CodeBlock code="<blockquote>This is a quote.</blockquote>" language="HTML" />

<Browser content="<blockquote>This is a quote.</blockquote>" title="Blockquote" />

</Step>

<Step submit="Continue">

Inline marks change meaning inside a sentence: <CodeHighlight>{"<strong>"}</CodeHighlight>
for important text, <CodeHighlight>{"<em>"}</CodeHighlight> for emphasis,
<CodeHighlight>{"<mark>"}</CodeHighlight> for highlights, and
<CodeHighlight>{"<code>"}</CodeHighlight> for code snippets.

<CodeBlock code="<strong>Important</strong> and <em>emphasized</em>" language="HTML" />

</Step>

<Step submit="Continue">

Small details matter too: <CodeHighlight>{"<small>"}</CodeHighlight> shrinks side
notes, <CodeHighlight>{"<abbr>"}</CodeHighlight> expands abbreviations, and
<CodeHighlight>{"<address>"}</CodeHighlight> marks contact info.

<Browser content="<p><abbr>HTML</abbr> makes <small>small</small> things clear.</p>" title="Text Details" />

</Step>

<Step submit="Finish">

Nice! You can now structure headings, paragraphs, quotes, and inline marks.
Typography is the voice of your page — keep it clear and consistent.

</Step>
