<Step submit="Continue">

Beyond paragraphs and images live interactive elements: dropdowns, collapsible
sections, dialogs, and labeled controls. Let's build each one.

</Step>

<Step submit="Continue">

A <CodeHighlight>{"<select>"}</CodeHighlight> dropdown offers a compact list of
<CodeHighlight>{"<option>"}</CodeHighlight> choices.

<CodeBlock code={'<select>\n  <option value="apple">Apple</option>\n  <option value="banana">Banana</option>\n</select>'} language="HTML" />

<Browser content={`<select><option>Apple</option><option>Banana</option><option>Cherry</option></select>`} title="Dropdown" />

</Step>

<Step submit="Continue">

A <CodeHighlight>{"<details>"}</CodeHighlight> block hides extra content behind a
<CodeHighlight>{"<summary>"}</CodeHighlight> label — perfect for FAQs.

<CodeBlock code={"<details>\n  <summary>More Info</summary>\n  <p>This is the hidden content.</p>\n</details>"} language="HTML" />

<Browser content="<details><summary>More Info</summary><p>This is the hidden content.</p></details>" title="Collapsible" />

</Step>

<Step submit="Continue">

Dialogs, fieldsets, and labels round out the advanced set. A
<CodeHighlight>{"<dialog>"}</CodeHighlight> pops a modal,
<CodeHighlight>{"<fieldset>"}</CodeHighlight> groups related controls under a
<CodeHighlight>{"<legend>"}</CodeHighlight>, and
<CodeHighlight>{"<label>"}</CodeHighlight> names an input for everyone —
including screen readers.

<CodeBlock code={'<fieldset>\n  <legend>Contact</legend>\n  <label>Name: <input type="text" /></label>\n</fieldset>'} language="HTML" />

<Browser content={`<fieldset><legend>Contact</legend><label>Name: <input type="text" /></label></fieldset>`} title="Fieldset and Legend" />

</Step>
